ABOUT WUDHU
The washing that purifies you before prayer

Clean water, a set order and about two minutes. Follow the guided steps with pictures, learn what breaks it, and get quick answers to common questions, all drawn from the Quran, the Sunnah and the books listed under Sources below.

9 steps4 must-dos3 duas
A SHORT HISTORY
THE WORD
Wudu is the act, and wadu the water used for it. In Islamic law it means using clean, purifying water on certain parts of the body, as Allah has prescribed.
THE COMMAND
Allah set out the acts in Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:6): wash the face, wash the arms to the elbows, wipe the head, and wash the feet to the ankles. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “I have been ordered to make Wudhu when I stand for prayer.” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai)
THE KEY TO PRAYER
“Allah does not accept prayer without purification” (Muslim), and “the key to prayer is purification” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah).
THE PROPHET'S WORDS
“Purification is half of faith” (Muslim). On the Day of Judgement his community will be known by the shining marks that Wudhu leaves on their faces and limbs (Muslim).
PASSED DOWN
Companions such as Uthman, Ali, Abdullah ibn Zaid and Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with them) described exactly how the Prophet (peace be upon him) made Wudhu, and their accounts are recorded in Bukhari, Muslim and the other books of hadith.

Why Wudhu

2 min
INTRODUCTION
Purity before prayer
The key to prayer is purification.
In one line

In Islamic law, Wudhu is using clean, purifying water on certain parts of the body, as Allah has prescribed: the face, the arms, the head and the feet. The act is called wudu, and the water used for it wadu.

Source: Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "The Definition of Wudoo'"
اِنَّ اللّٰهَ یُحِبُّ التَّوَّابِیْنَ وَ یُحِبُّ الْمُتَطَهِّرِیْنَ
“Verily, Allah loves those who repent and those who purify themselves.”
Al-Baqarah 2:222
Wudhu is needed for
Every prayer, obligatory or voluntary
Touching the printed Quran
Tawaf around the Kaaba
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Wudoo' is a Prerequisite to the Following Acts"

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “Allah does not accept prayer without purification, nor charity from wealth gained unlawfully.”

Sahih Muslim 224

He also said: “The key to prayer is purification, its entry is the takbir and its exit is the salaam.”

Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah (graded sahih by Al-Albani, as cited in the Description of the Wudu')

No prayer is accepted without purification. The Prophet (peace be upon him) called purification “half of faith” (Muslim), and Allah told His Messenger to make Wudhu when he stands for prayer (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai).

He also taught that Wudhu is a way for sins to fall away from the one who makes it well, and that Wudhu leaves a shining mark on his community on the Day of Judgement.

Source: Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "The Excellence of Wudoo'"

“When a Muslim servant makes Wudhu and washes his face, every sin he looked at with his eyes comes away with the water, or with the last drop of water. When he washes his hands, every sin his hands reached for comes away. When he washes his feet, every sin his feet walked towards comes away, so that he leaves clean of sins.”

Sahih Muslim (hadith of Abu Hurairah)

“Whoever makes Wudhu and makes it well, his sins come out of his body, even from under his nails.”

Sahih Muslim (hadith of Uthman)
Source: Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "The Excellence of Wudoo'"

The 4 must-dos

Quran 5:6
ESSENTIALS
The 4 must-dos
The four acts named in the Quran.
“When you rise to pray, wash your faces and your arms to the elbows, wipe your heads, and wash your feet to the ankles.”
Al-Ma'idah 5:6
1Wash your faceHairline to chin, ear to ear, including between the beard and the ear.
2Wash your armsBoth arms from the fingertips, including the elbows.
3Wipe your headWet hands from front to back and back again, once. The ears go with it.
4Wash your feetBoth feet, including the ankles. Washed, not just wetted.
Also required, according to these books
The intention in the heart. Do not say it aloud.
Bismillah at the start. The books hold it obligatory (like one view of Imam Ahmad); other scholars count it as Sunnah.
Rinsing the mouth and nose, once as a minimum.
Wiping the ears with the head, which the books treat as part of the head.
The order of the Quran: face, arms, head, feet.
No long gaps, so that a washed part does not dry before you finish.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Minimum Required Acts for a Valid Wudoo'"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 20; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank)
WHERE SCHOLARS DIFFER

Allah says in Al-Ma'idah 5:6: “wash your faces and your arms to the elbows, wipe your heads, and wash your feet to the ankles.” The books understand this as wiping the whole head, and the elbows and ankles as included in the washing. Some scholars say a part of the head is enough, and a few Maliki scholars did not include the elbows. The Burbank book says the order of the verse is obligatory while others call it Sunnah, and some scholars do not require the washing to follow without a gap. Follow your own school and ask a scholar if you are unsure.

What breaks it

6 things
KNOW THE LIMITS
What breaks your Wudhu
If any of these happens, make Wudhu again.
THESE BREAK YOUR WUDHU
1Anything from the front or back passageUrine and stool, and also pre-seminal fluid (madhi) or prostatic fluid (wadi). The Prophet (peace be upon him) told Ali to wash his private part and make Wudhu after madhi.
2Passing windAbu Hurairah explained that “hadath” is passing wind: Allah does not accept the prayer of one who has broken his Wudhu until he makes Wudhu again (Bukhari).
3Deep sleepSleep in which you lose awareness (Ali, Abu Dawud). Light dozing, like the Companions waiting for Isha with nodding heads, does not break it (Abu Dawud).
4Losing consciousnessFainting, insanity, a medicine or alcohol that takes away your senses, because you cannot tell whether your Wudhu has been broken.
5Touching your private part directlyTouching it with your hand with no cloth between breaks Wudhu, for a man or a woman (Busrah, Tirmidhi; Abu Hurairah). See the note below on scholars who differ.
6Eating camel meatA man asked the Prophet (peace be upon him): “Should I make Wudhu after eating camel meat?” He said: “Yes” (Muslim). For mutton, he said it was optional.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Things That Invalidate Wudoo'"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "Those Things Which Break Wudoo'"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 21
WHERE SCHOLARS DIFFER

Scholars differ on: blood, vomit or a nosebleed (the books advise making Wudhu as a precaution); touching a woman with desire (merely touching does not break it, as Aisha's hadith show); touching your private part (the books follow the hadith that it breaks Wudhu, while the hadith of Talq ibn Ali, “it is only a part of you”, led some Companions and scholars to say it does not); and eating camel meat (some scholars do not hold that it breaks Wudhu). Follow your own school.

These do not break your Wudhu
Light dozing while sitting.
Touching your wife or husband without desire (Aisha, Bukhari and Muslim).
Getting impurity on your clothes or body. Clean it off; your Wudhu stays valid.
Touching the private part of an animal (Ibn Taymiyyah, quoted by Al-Sheha).
Feeling doubtful. If you are sure you made Wudhu and only wonder whether it broke, it has not broken.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Common Mistakes" and "In Cases of Doubt"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank)
Constant leakage

If you have constant leakage of urine, passing of wind or bleeding between periods that does not stop, make Wudhu once the time of each prayer has begun. It stays valid even if the problem returns during the prayer.

Source: 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lessons 19 and 21; What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "How to Purify a Sick Person"
Good to know

Intercourse or ejaculation with desire, and the end of menses or post-natal bleeding, require a complete ritual bath (ghusl). Wudhu is part of the complete ghusl.

Common mistakes

9 tips
AVOID THESE
Small slips that spoil Wudhu
Check yourself against these nine.
MISTAKE
Missing the heels

The heels and ankles are the most missed spot. Some Companions only passed wet hands over their feet, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) called out: “Woe to the heels from the Fire.” (Bukhari, Muslim)

Fix: Wash the feet and rub each heel as you wash it.
Source: Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), citing Bukhari and Muslim
MISTAKE
Water not reaching the skin

Nail polish, wax, paint or a tight ring can block the water. The Prophet (peace be upon him) told a man who left a spot the size of a dirham dry on his foot to repeat his Wudhu and his prayer (Abu Dawud). Al-Sheha says Wudhu done over nail polish is not valid.

Fix: Remove polish and anything that blocks water first, and move your ring or watch.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Common Mistakes in Performing Wudoo'"
MISTAKE
Saying the intention aloud

The Prophet (peace be upon him) never spoke an intention before Wudhu. Ibn al-Qayyim and Ibn Taymiyyah say the intention belongs to the heart and need not be uttered.

Fix: Intend in your heart, then say only “Bismillah”.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Common Mistakes in Performing Wudoo'"
MISTAKE
A special dua for each part

Some people recite a dua as they wash each part. Ibn al-Qayyim said nothing is authentically reported from the Prophet (peace be upon him) during Wudhu except “Bismillah” at the start and the duas at the end.

Fix: Say Bismillah at the start, and the shahada and the duas at the end.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Common Mistakes in Performing Wudoo'"
MISTAKE
Wiping the neck

Ibn Taymiyyah said there is no authentic hadith that the Prophet (peace be upon him) wiped his neck in Wudhu. Your Wudhu is still valid if you do not.

Fix: Wipe the head and ears only, and do not wet the neck.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha); 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
MISTAKE
Washing more than three times

The Prophet (peace be upon him) showed a man Wudhu by washing three times each and said: “Whoever goes beyond this has done wrong and transgressed.” (Ibn Khuzaimah)

Fix: Once is required, three times is the most. The head is wiped once.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Voluntary Acts of Wudoo'"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank)
MISTAKE
Wasting water

The Prophet (peace be upon him) made Wudhu with a mudd of water, about what two cupped hands can hold (Muslim). The Burbank book warns against leaving the tap running while talking.

Fix: Keep the tap gentle and use only what you need.
Source: Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "Economizing in the Use of Water"; What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha)
MISTAKE
Thinking impurity on your clothes breaks Wudhu

If something unclean touches your clothes while you are in Wudhu, you must clean it, but your Wudhu is still valid.

Fix: Remove or wash the impurity. You do not need to make Wudhu again.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Common Mistakes in Performing Wudoo'"
MISTAKE
Rushing and long gaps

Leaving a part dry, doing the steps out of order, or waiting so long that a washed part dries before you finish.

Fix: Go in order, and count each wash calmly.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "A Description of a Complete Wudoo'"

Socks and shoes

Masah
MASAH GUIDE
Wipe over your socks
Instead of washing your feet, when conditions are met.
When it is allowed

Wiping over leather socks and the like is a concession from Allah that eases our burden. The hadith about it are so many that they reach the level of mutawatir. Jarir ibn Abdullah (may Allah be pleased with him) saw the Prophet (peace be upon him) pass urine, make Wudhu, wipe over his socks and then pray (Bukhari).

If you are wearing such socks, wiping over them is better than taking them off to wash your feet.

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Wiping over Leather Socks and their likes"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
How to wipe

Wear the socks while you are in a state of Wudhu. Next time your Wudhu is needed:

1Wash the rest of your Wudhu as usual, up to the feet.
2Wet your hands.
3Put your right hand on your right sock and your left hand on your left sock, and wipe from the toes up towards the shin.
4Wipe the top only, once. Do not wipe the sole.

Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) said: “If religion were based on opinion, wiping the bottom of the sock would be better than the top. I saw the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) wipe the top of his socks.” (Abu Dawud)

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha); 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
Time limit
24hResident
72hTraveller

Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) said the Prophet (peace be upon him) set three days and nights for the traveller and one day and night for the resident (Muslim). Both books say the time starts from your first wipe after your Wudhu breaks. After it ends, take the socks off and wash your feet.

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "The Time Limit"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
WHERE SCHOLARS DIFFER

Scholars differ on when the time starts. The books above hold that it starts at the first wipe, which Al-Sheha calls the stronger view. Some scholars count it from the moment the Wudhu is broken after putting the socks on. Follow your own school.

Conditions
The socks were put on while you were in a state of Wudhu (Mughirah ibn Shu'bah, Muslim).
They are clean. If there is impurity on them, remove it first.
They cover the parts of the feet that are washed in Wudhu.
It is used for Wudhu only, not for ghusl (Safwan ibn Assal).
You wipe within the time limit.
Boots and thick socks count too.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "The Conditions for Wiping over Leather Socks"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
What ends the wiping
The time limit ending.
Taking off one or both socks.
Anything that requires a ritual bath (ghusl), such as janabah.

If your Wudhu breaks, that does not end the permission. Wipe again within the time limit.

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Things that Invalidate Wiping"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 18
Turban or headscarf

A man may wipe over his turban and a woman over her headscarf. Amr ibn Umayyah and Bilal (may Allah be pleased with them) saw the Prophet (peace be upon him) wipe over his turban and his leather socks (Bukhari, Muslim). Umm Salamah is reported to have wiped over her head cover.

The Burbank book, quoting Imam Ahmad, says a cap or prayer hat is not wiped over, because it neither covers the whole head nor is tied round it.

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Wiping over a Turban or a Hijab"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank)
Bandage, plaster or cast

If a part that you must wash is wrapped in a bandage, splint or plaster, wipe over it with a wet hand instead of washing it. You do not need to make Wudhu or ghusl before applying it, and there is no fixed time limit: you may wipe as long as you need the covering. Wipe all of it. If the wound has no covering and washing it would harm you, wipe the wound itself; if even that would harm you, cover it and wipe the covering.

The Prophet (peace be upon him) said, about a Companion who had died after washing a head wound: “It would have been enough for him to make Tayammum, or to bandage his wound and wipe over the bandage.” (Abu Dawud)

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Wiping over Splints, Bandages, and their likes"

Tayammum

Guide
DRY ABLUTION
When water is not an option
Tayammum takes the place of Wudhu.
What is it?

Tayammum is purification with clean earth. It takes the place of Wudhu, or ghusl, when you cannot find water or cannot use it. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that the earth has been made a pure place of prayer for his community (Muslim).

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Tayammum"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 22
WHEN IS IT ALLOWED?
1No water availableAllah says: “...and you find no water, then perform Tayammum with clean earth” (4:43). The Prophet (peace be upon him) told a man in janabah with no water: “You must use the earth, for it is sufficient.” (Bukhari)
2Water would harm youIf you are ill or wounded and fear that water will delay healing or make the illness worse (Quran 4:43; hadith of Jabir, Abu Dawud).
3You need the water for drinking or cookingIf using it for purification would leave you or others thirsty, make Tayammum and keep the water to drink. Ali gave this ruling.
4Danger in reaching waterIf getting to the water risks your life, honour or wealth, for example an enemy or wild animals near it.
5Extreme coldIf the weather is extremely cold, you cannot warm the water, and you are almost sure that cold water will harm you. Amr ibn al-As prayed after Tayammum on a very cold night, and the Prophet (peace be upon him) laughed and said nothing against it (Hakim).
How to make Tayammum

Strike the earth, then wipe your face and hands:

1Make the intention in your heart to purify yourself, and say “Bismillah”.
2Strike the earth once with both palms, with your fingers spread. You may blow off the extra dust.
3Wipe your face with your palms.
4Wipe the backs of both hands up to the wrists: the left palm over the right hand, then the right palm over the left hand.

Ammar ibn Yasir said the Prophet (peace be upon him) told him: “It was enough for you to strike the earth with your two hands, then blow, then wipe your face and hands.” (Muslim)

Anything that is part of the surface of the earth works: dust, sand or a wall with dust on it. If you cannot strike the ground, you may put earth in a container or tissue.

Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "A Description of the Tayammum"; 33 Lessons for Every Muslim, Lesson 22
Good to know
Whatever breaks Wudhu also breaks Tayammum, and so does finding water or the reason for it ending.
If you prayed after Tayammum and then find water, you do not need to repeat that prayer (the hadith of the two men, Abu Sa'id, Hakim).
If you made Tayammum instead of ghusl, you must make ghusl once you can use water (Imran ibn Husayn, Bukhari).
One Tayammum can serve for the next prayer too, as long as nothing has broken it.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Things that Invalidate Tayammum" and "How to Purify a Sick Person"

8 Sunnah acts

EXTRA REWARD, NOT REQUIRED
1Begin with the rightThe Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “When you dress and when you make Wudhu, begin with the right.” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai)
2Wash three timesOnce is required, two or three is Sunnah. The head is wiped once.
3Use a siwak“If it were not hard on my people, I would have ordered them to use the siwak with every Wudhu.” (Tirmidhi, Ahmad, Malik)
4Run wet fingers through your beard and between your fingers and toesAnas (may Allah be pleased with him) described the Prophet (peace be upon him) doing this, and he told Laqit: “Rub between the fingers.” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi)
5Rub the parts as you washAbdullah ibn Zaid saw the Prophet (peace be upon him) make Wudhu with two thirds of a mudd and rub his forearms.
6Move your ringThe Prophet (peace be upon him) used to move his ring when he made Wudhu (Ibn Majah).
7Use water sparinglyHe made Wudhu with a mudd and bathed with a saa (Muslim).
8Say the shahada and the dua at the endThe eight gates of Paradise open for the one who says it (Muslim).
9Pray two rak'ahs after Wudhu“No Muslim makes Wudhu well, then stands and prays two rak'ahs with his heart and face, except that Paradise is due to him.” (Muslim)
10Dry your face and arms if you likeIt is permitted. Aisha reported that the Prophet (peace be upon him) had a cloth to dry himself with after Wudhu. Some scholars dislike it; the Burbank book concludes it is allowed.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "Voluntary Acts of Wudoo'"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "Other Aspects of Wudoo'"
When Wudhu is recommended
Before going to sleep, then lie on your right side and say the dua of the Prophet (peace be upon him) (Bara ibn Azib, Bukhari).
If you are in janabah and want to eat, drink or sleep (Ammar ibn Yasir, Tirmidhi).
Before having intercourse again (Muslim).
Before ghusl, as a complete Wudhu first (Aisha, Muslim).
For each prayer. The Burbank book says this is recommended and that praying all the prayers with one Wudhu is also allowed.
Source: What Every Muslim Must Know about Purification (Al-Sheha), "When Wudoo' is Recommended"; Description of the Wudu' of the Prophet (Burbank), "The Desirability of Making Wudoo' for Each Prayer"
WHERE SCHOLARS DIFFER

Ibn Taymiyyah, quoted by Al-Sheha, says it is not recommended to renew your Wudhu when you are already pure and have not prayed in between. The Burbank book says making Wudhu for every prayer is recommended. Both agree that one Wudhu is enough for several prayers while it is not broken.

Dua after Wudhu

3 duas
SAY IT DAILY
Finish with these words
Right after your last step. Three authentic words to say.
1 · THE SHAHADA
أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لا إِلَـٰهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لا شَرِيكَ لَـهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
Ashhadu alla ilaha illallah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluhu.
“I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger.”
The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that the eight gates of Paradise are opened for the one who makes Wudhu well and says this, so he may enter by whichever he wishes (Muslim).
2 · THE DUA
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِنَ التَّوَّابِينَ وَاجْعَلْنِي مِنَ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
Allahummaj'alni minat-tawwabeena waj'alni minal-mutatahhireen.
“O Allah, make me among those who repent, and make me among those who purify themselves.”
3 · ANOTHER AUTHENTIC DUA
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ أَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ
Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika, ashhadu an la ilaha illa ant, astaghfiruka wa atubu ilayk.
“Glory and praise are Yours, O Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but You. I seek Your forgiveness and turn to You in repentance.”
The shahada: Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai and Ibn Majah. The dua: Tirmidhi, graded sahih by Al-Albani. The third dua: Nasai.
