Salah basics

Why Do
We Pray?

The importance and purpose of Salah.

Allah says in the Quran:
“Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing.”
(Surah Al-Ankabut 29:45)
Prayer is important because:
It connects you with Allah 5 times a day.
It brings peace to your heart.
It keeps you away from bad deeds.
It is the first thing we will be asked about on the Day of Judgment.
It is a pillar of Islam.
Remember:
Salah is not just an obligation, it’s a gift from Allah.

How to
Perform Wudu

Step-by-step guide.

1
Make the intention (Niyyah)
Intend in your heart to purify yourself for prayer.
2
Wash your hands
Wash both hands up to the wrists, 3 times.
3
Rinse your mouth
Rinse your mouth thoroughly, 3 times.
4
Clean your nose
Sniff water into the nose and blow it out, 3 times.
5
Wash your face
Wash the entire face from forehead to chin, ear to ear, 3 times.
6
Wash your arms
Wash right arm then left up to the elbows, 3 times.
7
Wipe your head
Wipe over your head once with wet hands.
8
Wipe your ears
Wipe the insides and backs of your ears.
9
Wash your feet
Wash right foot then left, up to the ankles, 3 times.

Salah basics

How to
Pray (Salah)

Learn the positions and recitations.

Salah has 5 daily prayers
Fajr2 rak’ah
Zuhr4 rak’ah
Asr4 rak’ah
Maghrib3 rak’ah
Isha4 rak’ah
Basic positions in Salah
1
Standing (Qiyam)
Recite Al-Fatiha and another Surah.
2
Ruku (Bowing)
Say “Subhana Rabbiyal Azeem”.
3
Sujood (Prostration)
Say “Subhana Rabbiyal A’la”.
4
Sitting (between Sujood)
Say “Rabbighfir li”.
5
Tashahhud (Sitting)
Recite the Tashahhud, then the Salawat and a dua.
The prayer in 6 steps
1
Stand and intend
Face the Qibla and intend in your heart.
2
Say Allahu Akbar
Raise your hands and begin.
3
Recite
Al-Fatiha, then a short Surah.
4
Bow (Ruku)
Say the tasbih three times.
5
Prostrate (Sujood)
Twice in each rak’ah.
6
Sit and finish
Tashahhud, Salawat, dua, then Salam.
Key recitations
Takbir
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allah is the Greatest
In Ruku
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great
In Sujood
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Glory be to my Lord, the Most High

Salah basics

Start with
One Prayer

Begin with Fajr and build from there.

Why start with Fajr?
It is the beginning of your day with Allah.
Angels witness the Fajr prayer.
It brings barakah to your entire day.
Small steps lead to consistent worship.
How to start
1
Learn Fajr (2 rak’ah)
It is short and easy to learn.
2
Pray at the right time
Fajr time starts before sunrise.
3
Be consistent
Try to pray every day.
4
Then add the next
Move to Zuhr when ready.
